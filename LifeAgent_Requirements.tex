\section{Vision \& Purpose}

\textbf{The Life Agent} is a personal AI system designed to act as Mahir's accountability partner, life coach, and data-driven advisor --- available 24/7 via Telegram.

The core insight motivating this system: most habit and productivity tools fail because they are \textit{passive}. The user must remember to open them. The Life Agent is \textit{active} --- it initiates contact, asks questions, notices patterns, and calls out inconsistencies based on real logged history. It cannot be bullshitted.

\textbf{What it replaces:}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item A personal trainer (fitness accountability)
    \item A nutritionist (food tracking and guidance)
    \item A life coach (goal setting, direction, tough conversations)
    \item A financial advisor (spending patterns, savings discipline)
    \item A study partner (CFA prep, books, learning accountability)
    \item A scheduler (time management and life planning)
    \item A journal (longitudinal record of where Mahir has been)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What it does not replace:}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Mahir's own will and decision to show up
\end{itemize}

The agent's value compounds over time. After 3 months of logs, it knows patterns the user cannot see himself. After 12 months, it can project where consistent behaviour leads.
