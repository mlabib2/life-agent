\section{Vision \& Purpose}

\textbf{The Life Agent} is a personal AI system designed to act as Mahir's accountability partner, life coach, and data-driven advisor --- available 24/7 via Telegram.

The core insight motivating this system: most habit and productivity tools fail because they are \textit{passive}. The user must remember to open them. The Life Agent is \textit{active} --- it initiates contact, asks questions, notices patterns, and calls out inconsistencies based on real logged history. It cannot be bullshitted.

\textbf{What it replaces:}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item A personal trainer (fitness accountability)
    \item A nutritionist (food tracking and guidance)
    \item A life coach (goal setting, direction, tough conversations)
    \item A financial advisor (spending patterns, savings discipline)
    \item A study partner (CFA prep, books, learning accountability)
    \item A scheduler (time management and life planning)
    \item A journal (longitudinal record of where Mahir has been)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What it does not replace:}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Mahir's own will and decision to show up
\end{itemize}

The agent's value compounds over time. After 3 months of logs, it knows patterns the user cannot see himself. After 12 months, it can project where consistent behaviour leads.

% ─── SECTION 2: PHILOSOPHICAL FRAMEWORK ──────────────────────────────────────
\section{Philosophical Framework}

The agent's advice and default reasoning should be grounded in the following frameworks. These are not static --- Mahir may update, add, or remove them over time.

\subsection{Andrew Huberman (Science-Based Optimisation)}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Morning sunlight exposure within 30--60 minutes of waking
    \item Delay caffeine 90--120 minutes after waking
    \item Prioritise deep sleep (consistent bedtime, no screens before bed)
    \item Fasted morning exercise where possible for alertness
    \item Controlled dopamine management --- avoid cheap dopamine early in the day
    \item Meal timing: avoid large meals close to sleep
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hamza (Discipline \& Masculinity)}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item No excuses framework --- results are the only measure
    \item Purpose-first living: every action either moves toward the goal or away from it
    \item Consistency over intensity: showing up at 60\% beats not showing up
    \item Social media and entertainment as tools, not defaults
    \item Self-respect through keeping promises to yourself
\end{itemize}

\subsection{General Principles}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item Non-negotiables are sacred --- they are the floor, not the ceiling
    \item Progress tracked in weeks and months, not days
    \item Comparison is only to past self, never others
    \item Discomfort is the signal that growth is happening
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Health \& Personal Profile Records (Phase 2)}

A centralised, persistent knowledge base about Mahir that is injected into every Claude call. Unlike daily logs (which capture what happened today), profile records capture who Mahir is --- his biology, medical history, and physical baseline. This is what separates a generic chatbot from a true personal advisor.

\begin{longtable}{>{\bfseries}p{4cm} p{10cm}}
\toprule
Record Type & Description \\
\midrule
Blood Tests & Full panel results (iron, testosterone, vitamin D, glucose, lipids, etc.). Agent flags values outside optimal range and cross-references with reported energy, mood, and gym performance. \\
\addlinespace
Body Composition & InBody / DEXA results: weight, muscle mass, body fat \%, visceral fat level. Tracked over time to show progress beyond scale weight. \\
\addlinespace
Medical Notes & Injuries, conditions, medications, allergies. Agent never recommends something that conflicts with these. (e.g.\ knee effusion $\rightarrow$ no heavy squats flagged.) \\
\addlinespace
Dietary Profile & Restrictions, intolerances, preferences, current approach (IF, clean eating, etc.). Set during onboarding, updated anytime. \\
\addlinespace
Baseline Metrics & Resting heart rate, VO2 max estimates, sleep baseline, HRV if tracked. Reference point for what ``normal'' looks like for Mahir. \\
\addlinespace
Upload Commands & \texttt{/record bloodtest}, \texttt{/record inbody}, \texttt{/record note}. Mahir describes or pastes results; agent parses, stores structured JSON, and confirms what it learned. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\textbf{Important boundary:} the agent frames health insights as ``based on your labs and the research, here is what this may mean'' --- not medical diagnosis. It connects dots (e.g.\ low iron + poor gym performance + fatigue) and suggests actions, but never replaces professional medical advice.

\subsection{Financial Tracking (Phase 2)}

End-of-day voice or text spend log. Not a full budgeting tool --- a pattern-awareness tool. The agent notices drift before Mahir does.

\begin{longtable}{>{\bfseries}p{4cm} p{10cm}}
\toprule
Feature & Description \\
\midrule
EOD Spend Log & Part of the evening review: ``What did you spend today, on what, and was it intentional?'' Voice note accepted. Agent parses and stores in \texttt{logs} table with type \texttt{finance}. \\
\addlinespace
Financial Goals & Monthly budget targets and savings goals stored in the \texttt{goals} table (tagged financial). Agent tracks progress and flags drift. \\
\addlinespace
Pattern Detection & Agent notices recurring patterns over weeks: food delivery frequency, impulse categories, lifestyle creep. Raised in weekly summary when relevant. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\textit{Scope note: no open banking, no CSV imports, no category taxonomy. One daily sentence that the agent reasons over is enough.}

\subsection{Learning \& Study Tracking (Phase 2)}

Tracks what Mahir is studying and holds him accountable to it. Initially scoped to CFA exam preparation, but designed to generalise to any learning goal.

\begin{longtable}{>{\bfseries}p{4cm} p{10cm}}
\toprule
Feature & Description \\
\midrule
Study Log & Daily log: what was studied, how long, session quality (focused / distracted). One sentence minimum. Stored in \texttt{logs} with type \texttt{study}. \\
\addlinespace

\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
